## Documented feature contracts

### AI video editing

Descript links transcript edits to corresponding media cuts and rearrangements. Underlord performs broader AI-assisted editing across a project. These behaviors motivate a transcript-to-source-time map and an editable operation history in the clone; they do not reveal Descript's internal implementation. See [Edit like a doc](https://help.descript.com/getting-started/edit-like-a-doc) and [Underlord](https://help.descript.com/getting-started/underlord-beta-your-ai-co-editor-in-descript).

Editing operations have different effects that the clone must preserve:

| Operation | Documented behavior |
| --- | --- |
| [Filler words](https://help.descript.com/script-editing/filler-words) | English filler detection; remove corresponding media, insert a timing gap, ignore a match, or change transcript only |
| [Shorten word gaps](https://help.descript.com/script-editing/shorten-word-gaps) | Find gaps using a threshold and shorten selected/all results to a target duration |
| [Studio Sound](https://help.descript.com/effects-animations-transitions/studio-sound) | Adjustable file-level speech noise/echo processing; generated TTS requires conversion to an audio layer first |

Transcript correction, silence replacement, media deletion, and synthesized speech cannot share one ambiguous text-edit operation.

### Whole-video generation with voice

AI Video Maker accepts footage, a prompt, or a script, and creates an editable composition containing script, voiceover, visuals, and optional avatar. Documented output shapes are landscape, portrait, and square. The flow offers stock or styled AI B-roll and voice selection. This matches the starting dialog observed locally. See [AI Video Maker](https://help.descript.com/ai-assistant/ai-video-maker).

### Individual image/video generation

The generative-media pane creates assets for scenes. It exposes prompt, model, aspect ratio, video duration or image batch size, and model-dependent resolution/reference/audio controls. Capability support varies; disabled controls can represent unsupported inputs. Underlord can request media but does not expose all video-generation parameters. See [Generate AI images and video](https://help.descript.com/generative-media/generate-media).

The current model article lists image families including Flux Kontext, GPT Image 2, and Nano Banana variants, and video families including Gemini Omni Flash, Kling, MiniMax, PixVerse, Seedance, and Veo. The article is a vendor availability claim; these models were not exercised. Plan eligibility and settings vary. A clone should maintain its own verified capability registry rather than infer an API contract from these names. See [Generative models](https://help.descript.com/generative-media/models).

### AI voice and video regeneration

AI Speakers provide narration from typed text using stock or custom voices. The current speech guide names Multilingual v2, ElevenLabs v3, and v4; this documents offered speech options, not the entire backend architecture. It also distinguishes editable generated speech from converted audio layers. See [Generate text-to-speech audio](https://help.descript.com/ai-speech/tts).

Changing generated speech and regenerating a visible speaker are distinct operations. Descript's documented video-regeneration workflow has language, length, and speaker restrictions; it should not be treated as unrestricted text-to-video generation. See [Regenerate video](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/37015128387469-Regenerate-video-beta).

### Usage

Generation uses AI credits, and provider/model/settings affect consumption. No Descript credits or third-party API spend was deliberately initiated during this investigation. See [Usage accounting](https://help.descript.com/billing-payments-plans/track-and-understand-your-media-minutes-and-ai-credits).
